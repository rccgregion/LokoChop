# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

LokoChop actively maintains and releases security patches for the following versions:

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| 2.x.x   | :white_check_mark: |
| 1.x.x   | :x:                |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and integrity of LokoChop and our partner restaurants very seriously. If you discover a vulnerability or security issue:

1. **Do NOT publicly disclose the issue** via GitHub Issues, public social media, or public discussion channels.
2. **Email your findings** to `security@lokochop.ng` or contact our central security team via encrypted WhatsApp at `+2349074072454`.
3. Please include:
   - A detailed description of the vulnerability.
   - Exact steps or code sample to reproduce the behavior.
   - The potential impact on customer data, vendor bank accounts, or rider communications.

---

## Response Timeline

- **Initial Acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours.
- **Triage & Assessment**: Within 48 hours.
- **Patch & Deployment**: High-severity vulnerabilities are patched and deployed within 72 hours of confirmation.

---

## Data Privacy & NDPR

LokoChop adheres strictly to the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR 2019)**. We do not store sensitive payment card information, CVVs, or online banking passwords. Direct bank transfers occur strictly within the customer's regulated banking app.
